How it works, for the QML port: the scene is drawn once to an offscreen buffer, then a blurred copy of it becomes the fog layer. Droplets punch holes in that fog layer, so a clear streak is literally the sharp scene showing through. Each drop then re-samples the sharp buffer, magnified and flipped, inside a circular clip — that's the lens. In Quickshell you'd do the same with a ShaderEffect over a ShaderEffectSource of the scene, with the drops as a texture of positions; canvas here is just the cheap way to prove the look.
