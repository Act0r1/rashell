## Ayu terminal palettes

Ayu Dark and Mirage terminal colors use [ayu-colors 9.0.0](https://github.com/ayu-theme/ayu-colors) and the ANSI mapping from [vscode-ayu](https://github.com/ayu-theme/vscode-ayu/blob/444ef92911cb75c3933c8003e3a7c79b6b6c914f/src/template.ts), under the MIT License. The palette data and license are included in `assets/themes/ayu/`.

## Tokyo Night terminal palettes

Tokyo Night and Tokyo Storm terminal ANSI, syntax colors, and Ghostty settings are adapted from [tokyonight.nvim](https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim), used under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Its license is included in `assets/themes/tokyonight/LICENSE`. Base16 roles, search colors, and readable comments are adapted for Rashell.

## Ayu editor syntax colors

Ayu Dark and Ayu Mirage syntax role colors use [ayu-colors 8.0.1](https://github.com/ayu-theme/ayu-colors/tree/v8.0.1/src), under the MIT License included in `assets/themes/ayu/LICENSE`. Comment and punctuation colors are adapted for readability on Rashell's terminal backgrounds.
